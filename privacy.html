Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GTA6 Videos ("the App") handles information when its operator connects social media accounts to publish video clips.

1. Information we collect

When an account is connected, the App receives from the platform (TikTok, Instagram or YouTube) only what is needed to publish videos:

  • Basic account information, such as account ID, display name and avatar.
  • Access tokens that allow the App to upload videos to that account.
  • Basic statistics of videos published by the App (for example, view counts), when the platform provides them.

The App does not collect information about viewers, followers or any other users.

2. How we use it

The information is used only to publish scheduled clips to the connected account and to show the status of those posts to the operator. It is never sold, shared with advertisers or used for any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

Data is stored on the operator's own computer in a private database. Access tokens are kept private and are only sent to the platform that issued them.

4. Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the account remains connected. Disconnecting an account in the App, or revoking the App's access in the platform's settings, removes the App's ability to use it, and the stored tokens are deleted. You can request deletion of any data at the contact below.

5. Third parties

Videos and their captions are sent to TikTok, Instagram and YouTube, which process them under their own privacy policies.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.
