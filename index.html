GTA6 Videos

Short gaming clips, published automatically.

GTA6 Videos is a personal tool used by a single creator to publish short vertical clips about gaming news to the creator's own accounts on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube.

How it works

  1. The creator generates short clips from long-form gaming videos using a self-hosted editing tool.
  2. The creator connects their own social media accounts through each platform's official login (OAuth).
  3. The tool uploads the clips to the creator's own accounts at scheduled times, with a title, caption and a link crediting the original source.

The tool only posts to accounts the creator owns and has explicitly authorized. It does not post on behalf of other users and does not collect data from viewers.
